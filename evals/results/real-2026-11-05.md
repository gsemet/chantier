## Findings

1. **No Inspector sent work back, in five CRs.** An earlier workflow returned about one verdict in
   four. The Inspector may be too lenient, or these CRs too easy. An eval with planted defects
   would tell.
2. **The Foreman could not start before `foreman set`.** It stopped and asked. Letting it record
   its own session would remove the wait.
3. **`git branch -d` always refuses after a rebase merge.** The Foreman's card asks for it. The
   Foreman stopped rather than force it. PR 25 fixed the card and was closed.
4. **`open`'s `@<folder>` breaks in PowerShell unquoted.** PR 27 fixed it and was closed.
5. **The project had no long gate written down,** though the card makes that a question for the
   user. `AGENTS.md` now names it.
6. **`cost` counted the reused session's earlier batch.** It now counts from the batch's creation,
   on Claude.
7. **The scenario showed 156 checks on Linux and 157 on Windows.** The cause is not known.
