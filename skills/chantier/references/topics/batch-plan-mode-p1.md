The mode question, asked at each opening with the plan, is in the series format with no `<short name>-Q<n>` reference: parallel, series (with the order) and stack, your recommendation marked `(recommended)`. Weigh whether the CRs are linked (a `depends.json`, or one building on another's change), the overlap of the files their specifications name (the same files or linked CRs point to a stack, independent CRs with disjoint files to parallel) and the cost of the local-gate rule by shape (once per covered PR in parallel or series, once on a stack's top, as the floor's "gates" term shows; the project's rules decide who owes it). Name the shared files behind it, as the plan names a same-lines wait.
