# The rounds exception

A CR handed back `blocked rounds:` whose last round failed on a red the batch caused (a restack or rebase onto another CR's branch, a CR merged or moved below; judge from the hand-back, `log <cr>` and `diagnosis.md`; in doubt, the user's word) gets its next round from you. The cap is the batch's own `settings.max_rounds`, never the CR's limit `status` shows: +1 per grant, each on its own cause, never above `max_rounds` + 2 (default 3: the 4th, then the 5th). You alone answer for the exception. Send the Foreman two orders in one message, `order rounds <cr> --note <limit + 1>` and `order note <cr> --note "rounds exception: <the event>; <the failing check>"`, and tell the user in the same message, no wait: `CR-<n>: round <k> granted under the rounds exception: <cause>`. The Foreman takes the CR with that note and no escalation. Every other grant of rounds stays the user's.
