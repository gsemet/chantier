# Shared understanding, size, the critic line

- Ask "grilled enough?" only after a complete restatement in the user's language: what changes, every decision from `02-answers.md`, what is open. It ends with one line `critic: yes, <why>` or `critic: no, <why>`. A correction is an answer, recorded at once. Never decide alone that a CR is grilled enough.
- The user may ask for or cancel the critic in the free answer ("specify, no critic"): record it. On the user's word that it is grilled enough, record `Spec critic: yes|no, <why>` with the answer.
- Folder above 10 MB: the restatement says so and one more question asks what to do at the end (keep the retained prototype zipped in the PR's archive; keep all in the planning folder; drop the non-retained versions at `close`). Record it; the specification carries it. A folder that grows during the batch is the Driver's Known gap, and you ask at the ready.
- Files from a Griller in a worktree (`files for <slug>: <paths>`): read them, place them in the CR's folder as received (a new item through `file @<its request>` first), tell the user the worktree may go only after, review a specification as the spec-writer's.
