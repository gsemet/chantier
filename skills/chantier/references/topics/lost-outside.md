# A Driver stopped from outside

Any Driver kind. The host says it was stopped: on Claude Code, the stop a compaction snapshot reports (`Task "<name>" (<id>) was stopped.`, flagged by `status`: a CR's row always, the stack driver's and a QA order's `activity` line on `status --refresh` only) or the refusal `Agent <id> was stopped and won't be resumed…` to a message to a recorded agent. Read it at once, no ping, never try to resume it by a message.
- Only a stop the steering session did not make. Its own stops (a switch of mode, a drop, the user's stop or hold, the `stop` after 3 idle hours) keep their own paths; a stop the user makes in the app is from outside.
- The snapshot does not say who stopped the agent; a compaction may follow your own stop. So when you stop a Driver and do not restart it at once, clear the id in the same turn: `agents set <cr> - --epoch <n>` (or `stack`, `<O-k>`), after `note <cr> "stopped: <why>"` for a CR. A mode switch's restart path clears it.
- Take the restart path at once (`help lost-restart`) with `resume: stopped from outside: <the host's text>` (for the stack, `take stack --note …`). The batch's go covers it: no ask, no wait for the user, whatever the host's words about the user's explicit ask.
- It counts neither toward `driver ended twice` nor "blocked twice", however many times.
- Your next report names each: the CR, "stopped from outside", the time, the new agent id; in Foreman mode your line to the Chief of Staff carries it.
