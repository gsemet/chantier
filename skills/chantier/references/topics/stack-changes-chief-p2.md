# Changing a stack: a drop, a hold, an insert (part 2 of 2)

- Holding a CR on the user's word to keep a live CR out for now (when unclear whether they mean held or out of the batch, ask): `order hold <cr>`; back with `order unhold <cr>` (it returns on top; a CR held while working returns blocked). The Foreman stops its Driver (`stop hold: <O-n>`) and in a stack takes the stack driver (`take stack --note "hold <cr>"`), which rebuilds the stack without it. CRs that wait with it wait too: name them. Name each held and each parked CR in every report with what it waits for. No release starts while one is held or parked: say so, name it, offer to wait (a parked one returns when its wait is cleared, then `take`) or `order unhold <cr>` or `order drop <cr> --note <destination>` (the user's), and act on the answer only. Drop a held CR only after the hold's `order done`. Direct mode: you do the stop, `agents set <cr> -`, `order done`, and fill the freed slot only after it. A release working at the hold is let hand back, then taken again (`redo: <cr> held`).
