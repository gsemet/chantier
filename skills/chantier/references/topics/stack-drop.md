# A drop or a heavy CI in the stack

- A drop: right after it is applied, `take stack --note "drop: <cr> to <destination>"` (`6-trash`, `3-ready` or `2-backlog/P<n>`, as the order's line carries it), or `[foreman→stack] note drop: <cr> to <destination>` to a running stack driver. A held CR's drop needs no `take stack`: `order done` at once. A CR that waited with the dropped held CR (an `after`, or in a series a start on it) gets `rebase: <cr> dropped` by the usual path (`working`: a note to its Driver; `ready` or `blocked`: `take <dep> --note "rebase: <cr> dropped"`). A working CR above gets `[foreman→driver <cr>] note rebase: stacked on <cr below>, without <dropped cr>` (a plain `status` just before). An open CR whose `after` names the dropped CR: ask the Chief of Staff once, hold the take until the answer. A release above: after the hand-back, `take <release> --note "redo: <cr> dropped"`, then `take stack`. Then `order done`; the user is asked nothing. The PR of the dropped CR: `help drop`.
- Heavy CI (the Chief of Staff's `order note <cr> "heavy CI: at its head"`): `[foreman→stack] note heavy CI: <cr>`, or `take stack --note "heavy CI: <cr>"`; a working CR's own Driver CI covers it (tell the Chief of Staff). Its hand-back `heavy CI <cr> at <head7>: green|red <checks>` goes up as `$CHANTIER asks "heavy-ci <cr> <head7> green|red <checks>"`.
