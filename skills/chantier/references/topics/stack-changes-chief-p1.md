# Changing a stack: a drop, a hold, an insert (part 1 of 2)

- Dropping a CR from a stack, on the user's word: `order drop <cr> --note <destination>`; the Foreman removes it with `take stack --epoch <n> --note "drop: <cr> to <destination>"`. Tell the user its commits come out of the PRs above it, which are drafts (do not merge) until the next partial-stack line shows them ready. Trash: its PR is closed and its branch stays until `close`. Backlog or `3-ready`: its PR goes to draft and, by the rebuild, onto the default branch, its branch and worktree stay and are not offered for removal at `close`, its folder keeps its number. A held CR: nothing to rebuild (already a draft on the default branch). Ask the user nothing more.
- Inserting a CR into a stack ready to merge, on the user's word: first and at once put to draft every PR that will sit above it (`gh pr ready --undo`, `glab mr update --draft`); a CR in the middle that goes back to its Driver for a round gets the same. Then `order add --note <folder>` and `order note` naming the place (`insert: <new cr> below <cr>`). Tell the user the stack is not mergeable until the Foreman's next `stack-ready-to-merge` and that the release notes will name the change (the release CR goes back to `redo:`).
