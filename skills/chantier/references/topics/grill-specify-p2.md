# Starting the spec-writer (part 2 of 2)

- The writer and critic take no slot or budget; their cost is on the grill line. `3-ready` keeps no priority; `triage <3-ready-folder> --priority Pn` puts it back.
- A Griller sends nothing routine. It may send a live seat session `[griller→chief-of-staff] <slug> specified` once the specification is final (after the critic, when it ran), and must for a running-batch CR. A CR marked `work/running-batch` gets no `triage`, even when the grill recorded the next batch; with no live Chief of Staff, tell the user the next one finds it on `status`'s `running batch:` line and adds it after reading its specification.
`help grill-critic` for the critic.
