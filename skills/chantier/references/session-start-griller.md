# Session start: the Griller

Read by the Griller at the start of a session, after [session-start](session-start.md), together with
[titles](titles.md), and again when the role changes to it.

A Griller never starts the board or the watcher, whatever its project's batch: only the Chief of
Staff does, and asks the user the one watcher-and-board question. The board and watcher lines a
Griller may meet (`board on <URL> ...`, `board already running ...`, `watch: ...`) are the Chief of
Staff's, and are in [session-start-chief-of-staff](session-start-chief-of-staff.md).
