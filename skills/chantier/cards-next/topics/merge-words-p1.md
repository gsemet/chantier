# The words to the user about a merge (part 1 of 2)

The CLI's words, in the user's language: "ready to merge" only for a PR the user can merge now; "PR in draft" for a draft, with what it waits for; "ready locally" for done work with no PR yet. "Ready" alone is never said of a CR or PR, and "mergeable" never of a draft, whatever the forge's `mergeable` field says. An icon beside a CR is copied from `label` (🟢 ready to merge, 🟤 PR in draft, 🟣 ready locally), never judged, and is the present state; the future is said in words ("🟤 #<pr> CR-<n>, do not merge: it waits for its gate", never "the next ready to merge will be 🟢 #<pr>" while it is a draft).
- A draft is named with "do not merge" and what blocks it; a draft on another CR's branch is never "ready", even of its own work: merging it lands it in that branch, not the default branch. A draft's icon is its CR's state from the refreshed `STATUS.md`: 🟤 only with nothing left to do, 🟡 rebasing or running CI or a Verifier, 🟠 waiting for another CR's work (not its merge), 🔴 blocked.
