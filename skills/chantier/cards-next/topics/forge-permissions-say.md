# What the user hears of the forge

One block in the first message after the P5 and P10 lines: what may stop an unattended batch (a P10 warn first), each line with what to change in the forge's own words (for example a ruleset refusing force pushes on `chantier/*`: allow them for `chantier/**`, or exclude that pattern), the host's auto-mode alert sentence, and the release publication when the procedure runs it. A P10 warn is a question, asked once per session. Without a grant the host asks, by design (not a missing rule); with a grant its rules are offered (`help allow-rules`), and when they are not added the strong warning says the release may stop at publication. When the host refuses: "the publication is yours: you will be asked to run `<the command>`", said now, not at the end. Step 1 repeats only what is still missing.
