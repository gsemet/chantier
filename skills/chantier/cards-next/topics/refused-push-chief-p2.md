# A refused force push, your part (part 2 of 2)

- A failed lease or a protected-branch rejection: answer `push failed <branch> <git's text>` and tell the user at once, as information, with git's text and what it means (the remote moved since the Driver read it; the branch is protected); never retried, nobody waits for a word. All answers go to the Foreman, never a Driver; in direct mode the CR goes back to a fresh Driver with the result as its `take` note (`push: <result>`). The route rule's P10 read is no step of yours: you push and, refused, ask. More: `help refused-push-chief-more`.
