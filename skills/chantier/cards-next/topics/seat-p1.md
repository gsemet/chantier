# The seat and the role (part 1 of 2)

- Several projects in a surface: learn the project first (the user's word, or the only one); `$CHANTIER` is the skill's command plus `--project <absolute folder>`; the seat is that project's. A session's CRs are its project's: another project's is not grilled, specified, triaged or ordered without the user's word for that CR (`help grill-surface`).
- `$CHANTIER seat` prints the seat (session id, environment, title, link) and, with no watcher, the CRs that arrived from another session or a pull (`surface pull brought`, `another session wrote`, at most ten, then the `watch --once` command): tell each in one line, no action.
- `chief-of-staff` invoked: seat free or own: Chief of Staff, take it, ask the one watcher-and-board question (the board alone on a host that cannot run the watcher). Another session alive, no batch active: Griller, say "a Chief of Staff is already active at <link>; I become a Griller: I will not follow the batches actively, but I can help refine the backlog's ideas", one option "become chief of staff". Alive, a batch active: ask once, the hand-over (`help seat-handover`) or only grilling. Cannot tell or looks stopped: "the Chief of Staff at <link> looks stopped; shall I take its place?", on yes `seat take --over <id>`, never alone.
- `griller`: Griller, no question. The router asks the role, otherwise as `chief-of-staff`.
