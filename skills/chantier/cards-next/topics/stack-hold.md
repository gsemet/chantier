# Hold, park, a conflict that needs code, heavy CI

- `hold: <cr>` (its base is now the default branch): turn every PR above it to draft first (`stack --draft`); take its commits out of each CR above with no live Driver, bottom up (`help stack-changes`, the drop's `rebase --onto`); rebase the held branch on `origin/<default branch>`, push, `ship`; turn its PR to draft (`stack --draft <cr>`; `stack --out-of-draft` refuses a held CR); `$CHANTIER stack <cr>…` the rest (held apart), `forge-stack rebuild` (it moves the held PR to the default branch, `held out of the stack`); read the five checks, the local gate at the top if no pass, the chain check, the mergeable part ready again, the held CR never named. A held CR at the top has nothing above it.
- `park: <cr>` (an outside wait): the same for it and its dependents (`parked out of the stack`, `waits with`). When it returns it joins on top as a working CR.
- A CR held while working (`order hold` of a live CR; `claim lost`): skip each CR above that depends on it (it leaves with it); never rebase the held CR (its stopped Driver's work, its next Driver rebases it); its PR goes to draft and, by the rebuild, to the default branch as it stands.
- `unhold: <cr>` from ready: it joins like a ready CR (`help stack-joins`). From live: the next `take stack` prints it `not on its place`; the Foreman sends its Driver `rebase: stacked on <cr below>`; it is ordered on top as it hands back. The final-order take orders only the CRs of `chain`.
- A conflict that needs code, at a join or move of a CR with no live Driver: `git -C <worktree> rebase --abort`, push nothing for it, turn its PR to draft, place it on top among the drafts (the release last), go on with the rest including the `rebuild`, and before the hand-back send `[stack→foreman] anomaly <cr> conflict: stacked on <cr below>: <files>`.
- `heavy CI: <cr>`: run the project's heavy CI at that PR's pushed head by the means its rules give (a re-run, a label, a dispatch), or only read the result when the forge ran it. Judge by `help ci`, note `CI: gh pr checks <n> at <head7>: …`, and hand back `heavy CI <cr> at <head7>: green|red <checks>`. A red result changes nothing by itself. By default once at the top.
