# Changing a stack: a drop, a hold, an insert (part 2 of 2)

- Holding a CR on the user's word to keep a live CR out for now (when unclear whether they mean held or out of the batch, ask): `order hold <cr>`; back with `order unhold <cr>` (a CR held while working returns blocked). The Foreman stops its Driver (`stop hold: <O-n>`) and in a stack takes the stack driver. CRs that wait with it wait too: name them. Name each held and each parked CR in every report with what it waits for. No release starts while one is held or parked: say so, name it, offer `order unhold <cr>` or `order drop <cr>` with the user's destination, and act on the answer only. Drop a held CR only after the hold's `order done`. Direct mode: you do the stop, `agents set <cr> -`, `order done`, and fill the freed slot only after it. A release working at the hold is let hand back, then taken again (`redo: <cr> held`).
- Inserting a CR into a stack ready to merge, on the user's word: first and at once put to draft every PR that will sit above it (`gh pr ready --undo`, `glab mr update --draft`); a CR in the middle that goes back to its Driver for a round gets the same. Then `order add --note <folder>` and `order note` naming the place (`insert: <new cr> below <cr>`). Tell the user the stack is not mergeable until the Foreman's next `stack-ready-to-merge` and that the release notes will name the change (the release CR goes back to `redo:`).
