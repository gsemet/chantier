# The stack driver: other ends

- `hand-back blocked context: …`, or an end with no hand-back and no `activity`: no anomaly, no ask: `agents set stack - --epoch <n>`, `take stack --note "resume: <its text>" --epoch <n>`, a new stack driver on the batch's model; a failed restack is its `conflict-resolver` on the escalation model.
- `anomaly <cr> local gate failed at <head7>: <log path>`: no ask; `take <cr> --epoch <n> --note "local gate failed at <head7>: <log path>"`; it joins at a later take.
- `anomaly <cr> conflict: stacked on <cr>: <files>`: before the five checks, `take <cr> --note "rebase: stacked on <cr below>: conflict in <files>"` (the CR is `working`, its draft no failed check; its Driver rebases and it turns ready at a later take).
