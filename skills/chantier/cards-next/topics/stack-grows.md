# The stack grows

- The first take, once two CRs are handed back `ready` with a PR (neither depending on an open CR not ready): `take` starts each new CR with no open dependency on the nearest earlier CR with a PR once that CR is `ready`, else on the default branch, and a CR with an open dependency on its highest open dependency's branch (the CLI computes it, recorded at its first take). It leaves out a CR the journal shows merged (`forge saw merged`, not recorded yet) and prints a `base:` line naming it: pass that base unchanged, never correct a base by a note; the `order merged` stays the Chief of Staff's. While it is due, `status` prints `stack: take stack now: <crs> ...`: take it in that turn (only for the batch's first stack).
- Each later take, one for everything that happened while no stack driver ran: a CR got its first PR, a working CR rebased onto its place, a CR was handed back `ready`, a CR the stack driver's hand-back named `to come: <cr below> not shipped at <tip7>` (once its `ship` records a new head), a merge was recorded. Events while a stack driver runs wait for its end, except a merge.
- A merge recorded while a stack driver runs: `[foreman→stack] note merged: <cr>…` instead of a new `take stack`; while none runs, one `take stack` for what remains.
The later steps: `help stack-takes`; `deliver by <time>`: `help stack-deliver`.
