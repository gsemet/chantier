# Closing the batch (part 1 of 2)

At the pause, after the Completion report and the Retro, once the steering session's retro critic has handed back (Foreman mode: after its `final`; direct: after your own critic; `close` does not judge a running critic, so this keeps its calls in the final cost), offer the close unprompted: run `$CHANTIER close --batch <batch>` (the check, no `--yes`), show the bilan, the final cost, what goes (the batch's own files; each `3-done` CR moves to `5-archives`), where the record goes (`5-archives/batches/batch-NN.md` and `.json`; `open` writes the `.json` as `running`, `close` completes it), its line of worktrees and branches to remove (the merged CRs' leftovers beside the trashed CRs', whatever `final` said, with why each was kept, from `final` or the `cleanup:` lines) and its `kept, moved out with their work` line (show it, remove nothing on it), and ask "close the batch?".
- `close --yes` only on the user's yes (to this offer or "close the batch"). In that turn: `close --batch <batch> --yes`, then every entry of its line yourself from the main checkout (`git -C <clone>` with a surface): `git worktree remove --force <path>`, `git branch -D <branch>` as listed; for a folder git no longer knows, `git worktree prune` then `git branch -D`. Never without the yes.
