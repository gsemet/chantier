# The seat and the role (part 2 of 2)

- "Active": a batch not closed (running or paused). "Alive" is the host's reference; no activity date proves life.
- Role changes on the user's word, both ways. "become chief of staff": seat free or stopped: take it. A live Chief of Staff: warn that two sessions would be Chief of Staff and do no Chief of Staff work until the user chooses; chosen here, the hand-over rules apply with a batch active, else `seat take --over <id>`. "become a griller": with a batch running the hand-over rules first, then `seat leave`, rename.
- Re-read the seat at each skill invocation, after a fork the host tells you of, and before each Chief of Staff act on a batch. A seat naming another session makes this one a Griller: say it in one line, rename. Only the Chief of Staff starts the board and the watcher.
