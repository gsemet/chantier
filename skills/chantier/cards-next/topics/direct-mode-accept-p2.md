# What you accept while you steer (part 2 of 2)

- A paused batch is not running: this lifts at the pause; your retro and its critic still run after the pause and before the close offer. At the pause run the fast-forward, cancel the armed wake, write the retro (`help retro-lines`, `help retro-critic`), give the Completion report, then the close offer (`help batch-close`); a pause that prints `every CR is merged or cancelled, not paused: <causes>`: do what blocks it, then the cleanup and `$CHANTIER pause --epoch <n>`.
- Inbox ideas may be grilled at the pause and added with `order add --note <folder>`; your first `take` sets the batch running again.
