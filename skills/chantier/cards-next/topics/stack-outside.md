# A stack batch: the other asks

- `--outside-stack "<reason>"` only on an open order of the Chief of Staff on that CR that carries the user's word for that CR alone (not a `shape:` note); the reason starts with the order's id (`O-<k>: <words>`); ask with the flag first, then `order done O-<k>`. Never to get past a refusal on your own judgement.
- A ready CR refused behind a working or blocked CR's draft PR waits (`next: parallel until 2 ready with a PR`); when the CR in front is blocked, your ask about it also names the ready CR behind it (`…; <ready cr> waits behind it`). The final-order take ends the wait.
- An order note `shape: through the stack` follows the shape line's `next`: with `take stack`, take it (`take stack --epoch <n>`, or `[foreman→stack] note shape: <cr>` to a running stack driver), then `order done`; with `parallel until 2 ready with a PR`, `order done` and wait.
