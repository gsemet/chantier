# The words to the user about a merge

The CLI's words, in the user's language: "ready to merge" only for a PR the user can merge now; "PR in draft" for a draft, with what it waits for; "ready locally" for done work with no PR yet. "Ready" alone is never said of a CR or PR, and "mergeable" never of a draft, whatever the forge's `mergeable` field says. An icon beside a CR is copied from `label` (🟢 ready to merge, 🟤 PR in draft, 🟣 ready locally), never judged, and is the present state; the future is said in words ("🟤 #171 CR-<n>, do not merge: it waits for its gate").
- A draft is named with "do not merge" and what blocks it; a draft on another CR's branch is never "ready", even of its own work. A draft's icon is its CR's state from the refreshed `STATUS.md`: 🟤 only with nothing left to do, 🟡 rebasing or running CI or a Verifier, 🟠 waiting for another CR's work, 🔴 blocked.
- "You can merge it" only after the `ready-to-merge` line (or `stack-ready-to-merge`) has reached you, never on a Driver's progress, a green CI or the forge's look. A merge before that line is recorded as any merge (`order merged <cr>`, tell the Foreman in the same message), no reproach.
- `status` prints a `handed back:` line per CR: say its icon, its full PR link, each gap, its `do not merge: waits for` text and its `owed before the merge` clauses in the user's language, never the prefix. More: `help merge-gaps`, `help merge-recorded`.
