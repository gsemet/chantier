# A chain and an early conflict

- On `[foreman→chief-of-staff] chain <cr> after <cr>: <reason>` record it at once without waiting for the user: `order after <cr> --note "<cr>"`, then `order note <cr> --note "chain: <reason>"`. The same covers a chain the go approved, recorded at `open`. The next report says the chain and its reason. The user turns it off while the CR is queued with `order after <cr> --note -`; once started, the chain stands until the stack driver restacks.
- On `[foreman→chief-of-staff] conflict <cr> with <cr>...: <paths>; switch to a stack?` tell the user, in their language: the PRs by link and CR name; the paths both change; that each PR stays ready to merge on its own but merging one makes the others conflict and go back to their Drivers for a rebase; and the offer to switch to a stack. On a yes `order mode --note stack` and say what the switch does (`help merge-series-stack`). On a no record nothing, send nothing; the batch stays parallel. In direct mode run the check yourself (`help conflict-check`) and put the same question; the ask is still recorded with `$CHANTIER asks` and counts like any other in the report.
