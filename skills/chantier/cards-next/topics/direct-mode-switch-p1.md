# A switch to direct mode (part 1 of 4)

1. The user's word only ("switch the batch to direct mode", "take over the batch"), on a running or paused Foreman batch, its Foreman alive or not. It needs nothing a Foreman needs (no preflight P1 or P2): it works under Remote Control and in a terminal, on both hosts.
2. Pre-check, before anything is stopped: compare this session's transcript id (the host row says where it is) with the batch record's `chief_transcript` (`batch.json`). If they differ, `$CHANTIER seat take <your session id>` (it records this session's transcript on the batch) and compare again. Still differing, or the transcript unknown: the CLI could not tell your claim from a Foreman's, so stop and tell the user why; no workaround.
3. Is the Foreman answering? `$CHANTIER foreman get`, `$CHANTIER status --refresh` (no epoch) for `heartbeat N min`, then `[chief-of-staff→foreman] ping`. Not answering: its heartbeat is late by the board's test (older than the batch's steering wake plus 5 minutes) and no `pong` came within 5 minutes; or the user said so (no wait). When its heartbeat is "stopped" (a loop it ended on purpose, or the batch's pause), the time in `heartbeat_stopped` counts as its last heartbeat.
