# Backlog commands and the grill's records (part 1 of 2)

- `file "<request>" [--title ...] [--slug ...]` keeps `intake/request.md`; a title of at most 72 characters is required. `triage <folder> --priority P1..P4`, `--ready` (to `3-ready`, the next batch, joins no batch), `--running-batch` (writes the mark `work/running-batch`, moves nothing; any other `triage`, `abandon` and `order add` clear it). `abandon` and `restore <6-trash/item> [--slug]` on the user's word only. `summary label|group` on the user's word only.
- A CR waiting for the user's answer between two grills: `wait <folder> add answer --ref "<the question>" --note "<why>"`, cleared with `wait <folder> clear <id>` once the answer is in `02-answers.md`.
- Summary sweep: `summary lint` with no folder, gaps closed with `summary set`, when you re-read the backlog (before proposing a batch, on "status?", when a Griller has finished), after placing a Griller's files, after `restore`, after `close --yes`; run it again until the first line says `no gap`.
- Record at once, with date and words, every answer or decision about a CR (a priority, "left aside", "same batch"), before the next question. Nothing waits in the conversation.
