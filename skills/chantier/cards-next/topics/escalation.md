# Escalation

Two routes, each to a new Driver told `take <cr> --note "escalate: ..."`, whose rounds' Coder starts on the escalation model `playbook --batch` prints:
- a CR blocked twice on trouble in the work: `escalate: blocked twice: <reasons>` (`rounds:` and `fix:` hand-backs count);
- the user's grant of more rounds after `blocked rounds:`: `escalate: rounds` (a grant under the rounds exception keeps the batch's model: `help orders`).
A `rounds:` hand-back covered by the rounds exception does not count toward blocked twice, and a CR with an `escalated:` note can still get the exception. Record each escalation with `note` and give it one line in the retro.
Once per CR, then the user: a CR that holds an `escalated:` note for itself and fails again stays `blocked` and goes up as `question <cr> …` (one more round, drop it, another approach). A CR with no rounds blocked twice goes to that question at once. A spent budget, a round limit, a question, an approval or an infrastructure failure is one ask, once.
