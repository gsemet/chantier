# A join, a draft turning ready, a merge, a deadline

- A CR that joins (it has a PR, a draft): order it on top (`stack`), have it rebased, then `$CHANTIER forge-stack rebuild` (GitLab: retarget). The PRs below stay ready to merge; the user may merge them meanwhile.
- A draft that turns ready to merge (its CR handed back `ready`): when it is the lowest draft, no `rebuild`; read the stack back and turn its PR ready once the five checks hold, the chain check passes and, under the rule, its local gate passed at its head, then `stack --out-of-draft <cr>`. With drafts under it: order it below them, rebase it and the drafts above (theirs by their Drivers' notes when working), `rebuild`, the chain check, ready, `stack --out-of-draft <cr>`.
- A merge during a take (`[foreman→stack] note merged: <cr>…`): `$CHANTIER stack` again, restack what remains on `origin/<default branch>` with the CRs being added (one pass), `rebuild`. A `rebuild` refused because a PR of the plan merged meanwhile is an `anomaly` with the exact text.
- `deliver by <time>`: keep the stack mergeable at that time. Turn ready only what is restacked, judged and green at the top before the time; leave the rest a draft on top. Never leave the stack unstacked or a rebuild half done: a `rebuild` that cannot finish before the time is not started. A local gate not passed by then does not hold the hand-back: `local gate <cr> at <head7>: not run` (or `failed`); a running gate is not stopped.
