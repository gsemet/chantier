# Steering a batch yourself (part 3 of 3)

- Nothing is sent to yourself: no `ready`, no `[foreman→chief-of-staff]` line. An ask is recorded with `$CHANTIER asks "<text>"` and told at once. A Driver's refused rebase or push is judged and run by you (`help refused-push-chief`); the CR goes back to a fresh Driver with the result as its `take` note.
- `close` stays the user's word. In direct mode only your Drivers count as something that runs (their transcripts on Claude Code, their live leases on Copilot), not your own session.
