# The conditional lines of a report's Notes

- A chained CR (queued after another by a decision, with no real dependency): one line with the chain and its reason.
- Every Progress report: one line with the floor and the delivery time, `floor: at least about <h> h left (lock <n> gates × <m> min; chain <d> levels × (<m> + <c> min); critical path <CR-n> → …); delivery not before <time>`, or `delivery unknown: <reason>` with the depth and the critical path. When that time moved an hour or more later, one more line with the earlier time and the cause. Beside it, `partial stack mergeable below <commit>`. No new column. The Completion report has none.
- A Driver's refused force push run since the last report: one line per push, `pushed for the Driver by <the Foreman | the Chief of Staff | the Chief of Staff on your yes> (<each step: refused: <exact command> -> <exact text>, or not tried: route rule missing>)`, from `handoff <cr>`'s notes, or "(no refusal recorded)". A push held for the user or failed was told at once; **For you** names a push question while it is open.
