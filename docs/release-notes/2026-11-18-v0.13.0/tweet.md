# Announcement thread for 0.13.0

## 1/

Each time I merged a PR of a batch, I had to go back to the Chief of Staff and type "I merged it" before anything moved on. This afternoon I merged the last change of a batch and said nothing: about 27 seconds later the session woke on its own, checked the merge on the forge and recorded it. That is the new watcher in chantier 0.13, still on trial.

The release also lets you take over a stopped Foreman's batch in direct mode, with its work in flight, and keeps the change archive out of your PRs unless you ask for it. No eval campaign ran for it: the notes say which changes no run has seen.
