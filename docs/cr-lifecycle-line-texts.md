# Life of a change request, in one line

A change request (CR) goes through ten steps, in one line, from your request to your merge, and each step names who acts. Intent: you. Grill: the Chief of Staff or a Griller, with you. Spec: the spec-writer. Spec critic, drawn dashed because it does not always run: the spec critic, when impactful. Driver: the CR driver, with its Coder, Inspector, gate and CI. Verifier: the Verifier. Ready locally, drawn dashed and rare, no PR yet: the driver hands back. PR in draft: the driver opens the PR. Ready to merge: the driver, or the stack driver, takes the PR out of draft. You merge the PR/MR.
